Why we built it this way: This is the centerpiece. Today, "Get a Quote" lands on a page with no form. Now it's a two-step form: step one asks about the fuel need (easy, low-commitment questions), and step two asks for contact details. Multi-step forms usually convert better than one long form. It deliberately does NOT ask for tax IDs or bank info. That belongs in the credit application, after a rep has qualified the lead.
