Event & Project Fueling

Power and fuel for events that can't go dark.

Festivals, fairs, film shoots, emergency response or a temporary work site: if it runs on generators or equipment, Blu keeps it fueled from setup to teardown. We plan around crowds, schedules and access.

Request Event services (847) 566-5536
Why Blu

What you get

  • Generator refueling. Scheduled top-offs so power never drops mid-event.
  • Flexible timing. Overnight, early morning or around-the-clock coverage.
  • Temporary tanks. Short-term storage on site when you need fuel close by.
  • Emergency response. Fast fuel for storm recovery and unplanned outages.[Confirm 24/7 availability]
